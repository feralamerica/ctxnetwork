Who operates it

Operator

Allyvia's own privacy policy names Clock Tower X LLC as the operator. Clock Tower X is registered with the U.S. Department of Justice under the Foreign Agents Registration Act (#7649) as an agent of the State of Israel, run by Brad Parscale, Trump's former campaign manager. The same privacy-policy text appears, near-identically, across at least nine sites in the network.

Who it cites

The citation ring

Network articles cite each other far more than they cite the outside world. In one 199-page DOJ filing of 14 network articles, roughly 97% of citations pointed back at sibling sites, and 25 passages referred to other sites' content as "our analysis." Allyvia footnotes Cognitura; Cognitura footnotes Paxpoint; Paxpoint footnotes Allyvia. The effect is a self-reinforcing loop built to read, to a machine, like independent corroboration.

Why it exists

The machine audience

The network's contract with the State of Israel, filed with the DOJ, commits to "deployment of websites and content to deliver GPT framing results on GPT conversations." The sites have almost no human audience. Their structure, cross-citation, research formatting, uniform authorship, points at a different reader: the search and AI systems that increasingly answer people's questions. Full analysis: Before You Ask ChatGPT About Israel.
